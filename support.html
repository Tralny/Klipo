排查同步Troubleshoot sync

添加并使用 Klipo 键盘Add and use the Klipo keyboard

1

打开键盘设置Open Keyboard settings

前往“系统设置”→“通用”→“键盘”→“键盘”→“添加新键盘”，选择 Klipo。Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, then choose Klipo.

2

按需要开启完全访问Enable Full Access only when needed

插入文字不依赖完全访问。复制图片到系统剪贴板时，需要为 Klipo 键盘开启“允许完全访问”。Text insertion does not require Full Access. Copying images to the system clipboard requires Full Access for the Klipo keyboard.

3

切换并选择内容Switch and select

在输入框中长按或轻点地球图标切换到 Klipo。轻点一条文字、链接或颜色即可插入；图片会复制到系统剪贴板。In a text field, touch and hold or tap the globe key to switch to Klipo. Tap text, a link, or a color to insert it; images are copied to the system clipboard.

使用“保存到 Klipo”Use “Save to Klipo”

1

打开系统分享菜单Open the system share sheet

在 Safari、照片或其他支持分享的 App 中选择文字、链接或图片，然后轻点分享按钮。In Safari, Photos, or another app with sharing, select text, a link, or an image and tap Share.

2

选择“保存到 Klipo”Choose “Save to Klipo”

如果没有看到该扩展，请滑到操作列表末尾，选择“更多”并把“保存到 Klipo”加入常用。If the action is missing, scroll to the end of the actions list, choose More, and add “Save to Klipo” to Favorites.

3

回到轻贴完成导入Return to Klipo to finish import

分享内容会先进入安全的待处理区域；回到主 App 后由轻贴统一写入历史并按你的同步设置处理。Shared content is held temporarily, then imported by the main Klipo app according to your sync settings when you return.

iCloud 同步排查Troubleshoot iCloud sync

确认使用同一个 Apple 账户Use the same Apple Account

各设备需要登录同一个 iCloud 账户，并在系统设置中为 iCloud Drive 和 Klipo 开启访问。Devices must use the same iCloud account, with iCloud Drive and Klipo access enabled in system settings.

确认轻贴内的同步开关Check the in-app sync setting

在轻贴设置中开启 iCloud 同步。首次同步或大量图片同步可能需要更多时间，保持网络连接并让 App 完成一次前台刷新。Enable iCloud sync in Klipo settings. Initial sync and large images can take longer; keep a network connection and allow the app to refresh in the foreground.

同步不可用时内容仍保留在本机Local history remains available

如果 CloudKit 暂时不可用，轻贴会继续使用本机历史；服务恢复后再尝试同步。If CloudKit is temporarily unavailable, Klipo continues using local history and retries when service is available.

Mac 自动粘贴Automatic paste on Mac

为什么需要辅助功能权限？Why is Accessibility permission needed?

双击粘贴需要把内容写回系统剪贴板、恢复上一个 App 的焦点并执行一次 Command-V。没有该权限时，仍可复制后手动粘贴。Double-click paste writes the item to the clipboard, restores focus to the previous app, and sends one Command-V. Without permission, you can still copy and paste manually.

授权后仍然不能自动粘贴Automatic paste still does not work

在“系统设置”→“隐私与安全性”→“辅助功能”中关闭再重新开启 Klipo，然后完全退出并重新打开轻贴。部分目标 App 可能限制模拟键盘输入。In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, turn Klipo off and on again, then fully quit and reopen it. Some target apps may restrict simulated keyboard input.

常见问题Frequently asked questions

轻贴会把剪贴内容上传到自己的服务器吗？Does Klipo upload clipboard content to its own server?

不会。轻贴不运营独立的剪贴板服务器。开启同步时，支持的数据通过 Apple CloudKit 存储在你的 iCloud 中。No. Klipo does not operate a separate clipboard server. When sync is enabled, supported data is stored in your iCloud through Apple CloudKit.

为什么键盘里看不到刚分享的内容？Why is newly shared content missing from the keyboard?

先回到 Klipo 主 App 完成导入并刷新键盘快照，然后重新切换到 Klipo 键盘。Return to the main Klipo app to complete import and refresh the keyboard snapshot, then switch back to the Klipo keyboard.

如何管理或恢复购买？How do I manage or restore purchases?

在轻贴设置的 Pro 页面选择“恢复购买”。订阅管理与退款由 Apple 处理，可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理。Choose Restore Purchases from the Pro page in Klipo settings. Apple handles subscription management and refunds through your Apple Account.

仍然需要帮助？Still need help?

邮件中请写明设备型号、系统版本、Klipo 版本、问题发生前后的操作以及能否稳定复现。请不要发送剪贴板中的敏感内容。Include your device model, system version, Klipo version, steps before the issue, and whether it is reproducible. Do not send sensitive clipboard content.
