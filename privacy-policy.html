Klipo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Klipo is a clipboard manager for macOS. We respect your privacy and design the app so that clipboard data stays on your Macs and, when enabled by you, inside your Apple iCloud account. This Privacy Policy explains what information Klipo processes, how it is used, and what controls you have.

1. Information Klipo Processes

To provide clipboard history, pinboards, search, sync, and paste features, Klipo may process the following information on your Mac:

  • Clipboard content, such as text, links, colors, image previews, and file paths.
  • Pinboards, tags, pinned states, retention settings, theme settings, shortcuts, language, and panel display preferences.
  • Local metadata used for display and search, such as source app name, app icon, file name, or file path.
  • Purchase and entitlement status used to unlock Pro features.
  • Your email address and message content if you contact us by email.

2. Local Storage and iCloud Sync

Klipo primarily stores clipboard history and settings on your Mac. If you enable iCloud sync, relevant app data is synced through Apple CloudKit to your own iCloud account so that it can be available between your Macs.

We do not operate a separate server to collect or sell your clipboard history. iCloud sync is provided by Apple, and the transmission, storage, and account handling of iCloud data are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and iCloud terms.

3. System Permissions

Klipo may request, use, or direct you to enable the following system permissions:

  • Clipboard access, to read and save content you copy.
  • Accessibility permission on macOS, to support global shortcuts, return focus to the previous app, and simulate Cmd+V for automatic paste.
  • Login item permission, to launch Klipo automatically in the background after sign-in.
  • User-selected file access, to read necessary information when you choose apps or files.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases, subscriptions, free trials, and purchase restoration are processed by Apple App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your credit card number, payment account password, or full payment credentials. Klipo only reads the transaction and entitlement status returned by Apple to unlock Pro features.

5. How Information Is Used

The information described above is used only to provide, maintain, and improve Klipo’s core features, including clipboard history display, search, pinboard organization, Mac-to-Mac sync, purchase restoration, troubleshooting, and user support.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be handled by third-party services only in the following situations:

  • When you use features provided by Apple, such as iCloud sync, App Store purchases, or StoreKit purchase restoration.
  • When you voluntarily contact us by email or open external links from the app.
  • When disclosure is required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid government request.
  • When necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the developer, or the public.

7. Your Controls

You can disable iCloud sync, clear clipboard history, adjust history retention, manage excluded apps, disable launch at login, or revoke system permissions such as Accessibility and iCloud access in system settings. Deleting the app or removing iCloud data may remove related local or synced data.

8. Children’s Privacy

Klipo is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Klipo changes, as legal requirements evolve, or for operational reasons. Updated versions will be made available through the in-app link or this webpage. The “Last updated” date indicates when the latest version took effect.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Klipo handles data, contact us at fewerapp@gmail.com.
